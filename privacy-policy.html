Warranty Wallet Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

Overview

Warranty Wallet (the “App”) helps you keep track of products and warranty dates. This Privacy Policy explains how the current version of the App handles information.

Information we collect

Warranty Wallet does not collect personal information, require an account, or send product details to a server. Product and warranty details you enter are processed locally by the App and are not transmitted to us.

The current version does not use advertising SDKs, analytics SDKs, tracking technologies, cloud sync, or in-app purchases.

Information sharing

Because the App does not collect or transmit personal information, we do not sell, rent, or share your information with third parties.

Storage and security

The current version does not maintain an online account or remote database. You are responsible for keeping your device secure and up to date. If a future version introduces local persistence, cloud sync, or another feature that changes how information is handled, this policy will be updated before that change is released.

Children’s privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App’s features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page identifies the latest version.

Contact

For questions about this policy, please open an issue in the Warranty Wallet repository.
